
# Part B: AI Overview Module Structure

## 1. Module Introduction

**Module Name:** Curious Mind: AI Overview

**Subject:** Artificial Intelligence (AI)

**Intended Learners:** Beginners who want to understand the fundamental concepts, applications, practical uses, and responsible use of Artificial Intelligence without requiring advanced technical knowledge or previous programming experience.

**Module Purpose:**

The purpose of this module is to introduce learners to Artificial Intelligence through clear explanations, practical examples, guided activities, and supporting learning resources. The module progresses from foundational AI concepts and machine learning to generative AI, real-world applications, responsible use, and practical AI skills.

## 2. Intended Learning Outcomes

By the end of this module, learners should be able to:

1. Define Artificial Intelligence and explain its relationship with Machine Learning and Deep Learning.
2. Describe basic machine learning concepts, including data, training, models, and predictions.
3. Explain the basic purpose and capabilities of generative AI and large language models.
4. Identify applications of AI across different industries and evaluate their potential benefits and limitations.
5. Recognize ethical concerns involving accuracy, bias, privacy, fairness, and human oversight.
6. Apply basic prompting, verification, and critical evaluation skills when using AI tools.

## 3. Prerequisite Knowledge

This module is designed for beginners and does not require previous AI experience or advanced programming skills.

Learners should have basic computer skills and familiarity with everyday digital technologies, such as search engines, online platforms, and mobile applications.

## 5. Learning Sequence

The module follows a progression from foundational knowledge to practical application.

Learners first explore what AI is and how it relates to Machine Learning and Deep Learning. They then learn the basic machine learning process before studying generative AI and large language models. After developing this foundation, they examine real-world AI applications and the ethical concerns associated with them. Finally, they apply their knowledge through prompting, verification, and critical evaluation activities.
