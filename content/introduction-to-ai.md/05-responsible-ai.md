# Topic 5: Responsible AI, Ethics, and Privacy

**Author:** Samantha Salvatrice

## 1. What Is Responsible AI?

Responsible AI means designing and using AI while considering
safety, fairness, privacy, and its effects on people.

A system can be fast and still be unfair or unsuitable.

## 2. Fairness and Bias

AI may reflect bias found in training data or design choices.

For example, a system trained on incomplete historical data
may perform less accurately for some groups.

Ways to reduce risks include checking data quality, testing
performance, documenting limitations, and providing human
review for important decisions.

## 3. Privacy

AI tools may process personal information included in prompts,
documents, images, or connected services.

Responsible practices include:

- Avoid sharing passwords or identification numbers.
- Do not upload confidential records to unapproved tools.
- Use only information necessary for the task.
- Follow school and workplace policies.
- Obtain permission before sharing another person's data.

## 4. Accuracy and Reliability

AI can produce incorrect, incomplete, outdated, or misleading
information.

Users should check important claims against reliable sources
and confirm that cited sources support the claims.

## 5. Transparency and Accountability

Transparency means making relevant information about a system,
its purpose, and its limitations understandable.

Accountability means that people and organizations remain
responsible for decisions and outcomes.

Responsibility should not simply be transferred to an AI tool.

## 6. Academic Integrity

Students should follow their institution's rules about AI.

Responsible academic use includes checking information,
writing in their own understanding when required, and
disclosing AI assistance when instructed.

Students should never invent references or claim to have
completed work they did not do.

## 7. Responsible AI Checklist

Before using AI, ask:

- Is the tool suitable for my task?
- Am I sharing private information?
- Could the answer be inaccurate or biased?
- Have I checked important claims?
- Am I following assignment rules?
- Is a person reviewing important decisions?

## 8. Key Takeaways

Responsible AI requires fairness, privacy, accuracy,
transparency, and accountability.

Human judgment remains important.
