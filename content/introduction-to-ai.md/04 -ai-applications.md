**Author:** Lai Quoc Hung

## 1. Introduction

AI is used in many industries to recognize patterns, make
predictions, organize information, and generate content.

Its usefulness depends on the task, the quality of the data,
and how people check the results.

## 2. Education

AI can help explain concepts, generate practice questions,
and provide language feedback.

Benefits:
- Personalized practice
- Quick feedback
- Support for different learning needs

Risks:
- Incorrect explanations
- Privacy concerns
- Academic-integrity problems

Students should verify information and follow school rules.

## 3. Healthcare

AI can support medical-image analysis, research, and
administrative tasks.

Benefits:
- Assistance with large amounts of information
- Potentially faster analysis

Risks:
- Inaccurate results
- Biased data
- Privacy concerns

AI should not be treated as a guaranteed diagnosis or a
replacement for qualified healthcare professionals.

## 4. Business and Finance

Businesses may use AI for customer service, demand forecasting,
fraud detection, and product recommendations.

AI may improve efficiency, but it can also make inaccurate
predictions or produce unfair outcomes.

## 5. Transportation

AI can support traffic prediction, route planning, and
driver-assistance features.

However, changing conditions and technical limitations can
create safety risks.

## 6. Entertainment

Streaming platforms may recommend content based on viewing
patterns. Generative AI can also assist with creative work.

Possible risks include privacy concerns, misleading synthetic
media, and issues involving permission or attribution.

## 7. Evaluating AI Applications

Ask these questions:

1. What problem does the AI solve?
2. What data does it use?
3. Who benefits from the system?
4. What could go wrong?
5. Who checks the result?
6. Who is responsible if an error occurs?

## 8. Key Takeaways

AI can support many industries, but every application should
be evaluated for usefulness, accuracy, fairness, and privacy.
