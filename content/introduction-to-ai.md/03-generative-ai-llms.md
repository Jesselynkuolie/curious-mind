# Topic 3: Generative AI and Large Language Models

**Author:** Kristabelle Lian

## 1. What Is Generative AI?

Generative AI refers to AI systems that create content,
including text, images, audio, video, or computer code.

These systems can help people brainstorm, summarize,
translate, draft, and explore ideas.

## 2. What Is a Large Language Model?

A large language model (LLM) is an AI model designed to
process and generate language.

Many LLMs generate text by predicting likely next tokens.
A token may be a word, part of a word, or another unit of text.

Fluent text does not guarantee that the information is true.

## 3. Common Uses

- Writing and editing assistance
- Summarizing documents
- Explaining difficult concepts
- Translation and rephrasing
- Brainstorming ideas
- Coding assistance

## 4. Writing Better Prompts

A prompt is an instruction given to an AI tool.

A useful prompt includes:

- Task: What should the AI do?
- Context: What information does it need?
- Format: How should the answer look?
- Audience: Who will read the answer?
- Constraints: What limits should it follow?

Example of a vague prompt:

"Explain AI."

Improved prompt:

"Explain AI to a first-year university student in 150 words.
Include two everyday examples and one limitation."

A detailed prompt may improve relevance but cannot guarantee
that the response is accurate.

## 5. AI Hallucinations

An AI hallucination occurs when a system produces false or
unsupported information as if it were true.

For example, an AI tool might invent a book title or provide
a reference that does not support its claim.

## 6. How to Check AI Answers

1. Identify important factual claims.
2. Check reliable, independent sources.
3. Open the cited sources.
4. Verify names, dates, and numbers.
5. Follow school rules on AI assistance.

## 7. Key Takeaways

- Generative AI creates content.
- LLMs work with language.
- Specific prompts can improve relevance.
- AI can generate false information.
- Important answers should be verified.
