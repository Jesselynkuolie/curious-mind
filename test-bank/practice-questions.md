Welcome back, Explorer. Detective Pip has hidden six AI mysteries across this Test Bank. Your job is not simply to answer questions — it is to catch weak reasoning, spot misleading AI claims, test your own understanding, and explain your thinking like a real AI investigator.

## Topic 1: Introduction to Artificial Intelligence

### Question 1
What is Artificial Intelligence (AI)?

A. Any electronic device connected to the internet.  
B. A field focused on developing systems that perform tasks associated with intelligent behavior.  
C. A computer program that only performs calculations.  
D. A technology that always makes better decisions than humans.

### Question 2
What is the relationship between AI, Machine Learning, and Deep Learning?

A. They are completely unrelated fields.  
B. AI is a type of Deep Learning.  
C. Machine Learning is part of AI, and Deep Learning is a type of Machine Learning.  
D. Deep Learning does not use data.

### Question 3
Which example best represents traditional rule-based automation?

A. A model learns to recognize cats from photographs.  
B. A recommendation system learns from users' viewing histories.  
C. A model predicts house prices using historical data.  
D. A calculator follows programmed mathematical rules to perform addition.

# Part D: Short-Answer Sample Answers and Marking Guide

The answers below are examples. Other accurate answers should also receive credit.

## Question 16: Understanding AI

**Sample answer:**

Artificial Intelligence is the broad field of developing computer systems that perform tasks associated with intelligent behavior. Machine Learning is a part of AI that learns patterns from data. Deep Learning is a type of Machine Learning that uses neural networks with multiple processing layers. An example of AI is an email spam filter that classifies messages.

**Marking guide:**

- 1 mark: Correctly explains AI.
- 1 mark: Explains Machine Learning as part of AI.
- 1 mark: Explains Deep Learning as a type of Machine Learning.
- 1 mark: Gives a relevant AI application.

## Question 17: Machine Learning

**Sample answer:**

Supervised learning uses labeled examples to learn a relationship between inputs and known answers. For example, an email filter can learn from messages labeled "spam" or "not spam." Unsupervised learning identifies patterns in data without supplied target labels. For example, a retailer can group customers according to similarities in purchasing behavior.

**Marking guide:**

- 1 mark: Correctly explains supervised learning.
- 1 mark: Gives a relevant supervised learning example.
- 1 mark: Correctly explains unsupervised learning.
- 1 mark: Gives a relevant unsupervised learning example.

## Question 18: Generative AI

**Sample answer:**

Generative AI is designed to create content such as text, images, audio, or code. One limitation is that it can generate false information or invent references. To check reliability, a user can verify important claims against reliable sources and confirm that cited references actually exist.

**Marking guide:**

- 1 mark: Correctly defines generative AI.
- 1 mark: Identifies a relevant limitation.
- 1 mark: Describes an appropriate verification method.
- 1 mark: Explains how verification helps establish reliability.

## Question 19: Responsible AI

**Sample answer:**

One concern is privacy because users might enter personal or confidential information into an AI tool. This risk can be reduced by avoiding unnecessary sensitive information and following applicable data-protection rules. A second concern is bias, because an AI system may produce unfair outcomes. This risk can be addressed by evaluating the system's performance across relevant groups and investigating possible disparities.

**Marking guide:**

- 1 mark: Identifies the first ethical or safety concern.
- 1 mark: Gives a suitable action to address the first concern.
- 1 mark: Identifies a second concern.
- 1 mark: Gives a suitable action to address the second concern.

Other relevant concerns, such as accuracy, transparency, accountability, and safety, may receive credit when explained correctly.

## Question 20: Practical AI Skills

**Sample answer:**

**Prompt:** "Explain machine learning to a beginner using simple English. Organize the response under three headings and include two everyday examples."

To verify the response, I would check the explanation against reliable educational sources, confirm that the examples are appropriate, and investigate any factual claims that seem uncertain. I would also check for missing information or misleading statements.

**Marking guide:**

- 1 mark: Includes a clear goal.
- 1 mark: Identifies the intended audience.
- 1 mark: Specifies an output requirement.
- 1 mark: Explains an appropriate method for verifying the response.
