
# Curious Mind — Review and Learner Testing

## 1. Purpose

This document records the peer-review feedback and testing status of the Curious Mind — AI Overview learning materials.

It combines feedback from two reviewing teams:
- Team DLLM

## 5. Overall Strengths Across Both Reviews

The reviewing teams identified several positive aspects:

- The project covered important introductory AI topics.
- The Study Guide included practical activities.
- Worked examples and calculations were generally accurate.
- The Test Bank included a variety of questions.
- Some PowerPoint slides were visually engaging.
- Video demonstrations helped explain practical AI skills.
- The learning materials showed connections between explanations, activities, and assessments.

These strengths should be preserved during revision.

## 8. Learner Testing

Formal learner testing of the Study Guide and Test Bank has not yet been documented in the available records.

Peer review and learner testing are different activities. The comments from Team DLLM and Team Bumingbai are recorded as peer-review feedback, not as formal learner-testing results.

The team should conduct and document learner testing before claiming it has been completed.

### Suggested Learner Testing Questions

1. Were the explanations easy to understand?
2. Were the examples helpful?
3. Were the activities clear?
4. Were the questions appropriate for beginners?
5. Which section was most difficult to understand?
6. What improvements would make the materials easier to use?

Record actual participant responses and feedback.

## 10. Conclusion

The peer reviews from Team DLLM and Team Bumingbai provided useful feedback on the Curious Mind learning materials.

Both reviews identified strengths in the project's content and learning activities while highlighting areas requiring improvement.

The main priorities are to complete unfinished documentation, improve visual support, strengthen assessment alignment, improve presentation coverage, and make video tutorials more effective for learners.

The feedback provides a foundation for the team's Stage 3 revisions and final verification.
