# Video Plan: Curious Mind — AI Overview

**Assigned to:** Kristabelle Lian

## Objective
Create a short introduction to AI for beginner learners.

**Target audience:** Students and beginner learners  
**Suggested duration:** 3–5 minutes

## Video Structure

| Time | Segment | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:20 | Opening | Introduce AI and everyday examples |
| 0:20–0:55 | AI Basics | Explain the meaning of AI |
| 0:55–1:35 | Machine Learning | Explain data, training, and models |
| 1:35–2:15 | Generative AI | Explain content generation and prompts |
| 2:15–2:55 | Applications | Show AI in different industries |
| 2:55–3:40 | Risks | Discuss bias, privacy, and accuracy |
| 3:40–4:10 | Closing | Summarize key takeaways |
