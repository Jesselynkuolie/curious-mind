# Video Plan: Curious Mind — AI Overview

- **Project:** Curious Mind
- **Video title:** Understanding Artificial Intelligence: A Beginner's Guide
- **Assigned video lead:** Kristabelle Lian

- **Target duration:** Approximately 4 minutes and 10 seconds
- **Acceptable duration:** 3–5 minutes, unless the assignment specifies otherwise

- **Status:** Planning and pre-production

## 2. Purpose

The purpose of this video is to introduce artificial intelligence (AI) in clear, accessible language. It will connect the concepts covered in the Curious Mind learning materials to familiar examples and encourage learners to use AI tools critically and responsibly.

The video will introduce AI, explain the basic idea behind machine learning, describe generative AI, show practical applications, and highlight important limitations and ethical concerns.

## 3. Learning Objectives

By the end of the video, learners should be able to:

1. Explain the general meaning of artificial intelligence.
2. Describe how machine learning uses data to learn patterns and produce predictions.
3. Explain what generative AI does and why prompts matter.
4. Identify examples of AI applications in everyday life and different industries.
5. Recognize risks involving accuracy, bias, privacy, and responsible use.
6. Apply three practical habits: ask clear questions, verify important information, and protect personal data.

## 4. Key Messages

The video should communicate the following messages:

- AI refers to technologies designed to perform tasks associated with intelligent behaviour.
- Machine learning is one approach to developing AI systems; not every AI system learns in the same way.
- Generative AI can produce content, but its outputs are not automatically accurate.
- AI can provide useful assistance, but its performance depends on the task, data, and system design.
- People should consider fairness, privacy, reliability, and human oversight when using AI.
- AI literacy includes understanding both the benefits and the limitations of these technologies.

## 6. Narration and Content Guidance

### Segment 1: Opening (0:00–0:20)

**Purpose:** Gain attention and connect the topic to everyday experience.

Introduce familiar situations in which people may encounter AI, such as navigation recommendations, content recommendations, and email spam filtering.

**Key question:** Where do you encounter AI in your daily life?

**Visual guidance:** Use simple icons or original illustrations. Avoid cluttered backgrounds and unnecessary animation.

### Segment 2: AI Basics (0:20–0:55)

**Purpose:** Give learners a clear starting definition.

Explain that artificial intelligence is a broad field concerned with systems that perform tasks commonly associated with intelligent behaviour. Clarify that different AI systems use different techniques and capabilities.

**Key message:** AI is a broad category of technologies, not one single tool or method.

**Visual guidance:** Display a short definition alongside two or three familiar examples.

### Segment 3: Machine Learning (0:55–1:35)

**Purpose:** Explain the basic machine-learning process.

Use spam detection to illustrate how a machine-learning system can learn patterns from examples and apply a trained model to classify new emails.

Show the simplified process:

**Data → Training → Model → Prediction**

Explain that a model's performance depends on the quality and suitability of its data, training, and evaluation.

**Key message:** Machine learning can identify patterns in data, but its predictions are not guaranteed to be correct.

### Segment 4: Generative AI (1:35–2:15)

**Purpose:** Introduce generative AI and effective prompting.

Explain that generative AI can produce content such as text, images, and other media. Demonstrate how adding a task, context, audience, and output format can make a prompt more useful.

**Example comparison:**

- Vague prompt: "Explain AI."
- More specific prompt: "Explain artificial intelligence to a beginner in three simple bullet points and include one everyday example."

Clarify that a more detailed prompt may improve relevance and clarity, but it does not guarantee factual accuracy.

**Key message:** Clear prompts help communicate what you need; important outputs still require verification.

### Segment 5: AI Applications (2:15–2:55)

**Purpose:** Show how AI can be applied in different settings.

Use four concise examples:

- **Education:** Supporting practice, explanations, or feedback.
- **Healthcare:** Assisting with selected tasks, subject to appropriate professional oversight.
- **Business:** Helping organize information or automate selected workflows.
- **Transport:** Supporting route planning or traffic prediction.

For each area, mention a limitation or consideration. Examples include inaccurate outputs, privacy risks, unequal performance, and the need for human judgement.

**Key message:** AI can be useful, but its suitability depends on the task and how it is implemented.

Avoid unsupported statistics or claims that AI always improves outcomes.

### Segment 6: Responsible AI (2:55–3:40)

**Purpose:** Encourage critical and responsible use.

Cover four considerations:

1. **Accuracy:** Check important claims against reliable sources.
2. **Bias and fairness:** Consider whether a system may perform differently for different people or groups.
3. **Privacy:** Do not enter sensitive personal or confidential information into tools unless the use is authorized and appropriate.
4. **Accountability:** Use human judgement and appropriate oversight for consequential decisions.

**Key message:** AI outputs should be evaluated rather than accepted automatically.

### Segment 7: Conclusion (3:40–4:10)

**Purpose:** Reinforce the learning objectives.

Summarize the main ideas in three practical takeaways:

- Ask clear questions.
- Verify important information.
- Use AI responsibly.

End with the Curious Mind project title and any required team credits.

## 10. Quality and Accessibility Standards

The finished video should:

- Use clear, beginner-friendly language.
- Explain technical terms when they first appear.
- Present examples that support the learning objectives.
- Avoid suggesting that AI systems are always accurate or unbiased.
- Use readable text and sufficient contrast.
- Include accurate captions or subtitles.
- Keep narration clear and audio at a consistent level.
- Avoid unnecessary visual effects that distract from the explanation.
- Protect personal information and respect asset licences.
- Meet the duration and file-format requirements in the official assignment instructions.
