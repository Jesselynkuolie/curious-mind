## Topic 1: Introduction to AI

- **Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a broad field of technology.** It focuses on creating computer systems that can perform tasks that usually require human intelligence, such as understanding language, recognizing images, solving problems, and making predictions.

- **Machine learning is one approach within AI.** Instead of giving a computer every rule it needs to follow, machine learning allows it to learn patterns from examples and data.

- **Not every digital tool uses AI.** Some programs follow fixed instructions written by their developers. For example, a basic calculator follows mathematical rules, while an AI system may recognize objects in photographs.

- **AI can make mistakes.** It may provide incorrect facts, misunderstand a question, or produce misleading results. Therefore, users should check important information rather than automatically trusting AI.

## Topic 2: Machine Learning

Machine learning is an approach to AI in which computers learn patterns from data to make predictions or decisions.

- **Supervised learning uses labeled examples.** The training data includes examples with known answers. For instance, a system can learn to identify cats and dogs from photographs labeled with the correct animal names.

- **Unsupervised learning finds patterns without supplied labels.** The system examines data to discover groups or relationships on its own. For example, a business might group customers according to their shopping habits without providing predefined customer categories.

- **Reinforcement learning uses feedback.** A system learns by taking actions and receiving rewards or penalties. For example, an AI system learning to play a game may improve by receiving rewards for successful moves.

- **Overfitting happens when a model learns its training examples too specifically.** It may perform very well on the examples it has already seen but poorly on new examples. This is similar to memorizing practice questions without understanding how to solve different problems.

## Topic 3: Generative AI and Large Language Models (LLMs)

- **Generative AI creates new content.** It can generate text, images, music, and other forms of content based on patterns learned during training. For example, an AI tool can create a story from a written prompt.

- **Large Language Models (LLMs) work with language.** They learn patterns in text and can perform tasks such as answering questions, summarizing articles, translating sentences, and generating written responses.

- **Prompts do not guarantee accurate answers.** A prompt is the instruction or question given to an AI tool. A clear prompt can help the tool understand what the user wants, but it does not guarantee that the answer will be correct.

- **AI hallucinations are generated claims that are false, fabricated, or unsupported.** For example, an AI tool might invent a research paper or provide a false explanation while presenting it as a fact.

## Topic 4: AI Applications

AI is used in many industries to help people perform tasks, analyze information, and solve problems. However, every application has potential benefits and risks.

- **Education:** AI can explain difficult concepts, generate practice questions, and provide learning support. However, students may become too dependent on AI or receive incorrect explanations.

- **Healthcare:** AI can help medical professionals analyze medical images and identify patterns in patient data. However, incorrect predictions may affect patient care, so appropriate professional oversight is important.

- **Business:** AI can help companies analyze customer preferences, predict demand, and automate repetitive tasks. However, biased data or inaccurate predictions can lead to unfair or costly decisions.

- **Transportation:** AI can help vehicles recognize obstacles, plan routes, and assist with traffic management. However, system errors can create safety risks.

- **Entertainment:** AI can recommend films, music, and games based on users' interests. However, recommendations may limit exposure to different types of content or raise privacy concerns.

## Topic 5: Responsible AI

Responsible AI use means using AI carefully, honestly, and safely while considering its effects on people.

- **Fairness:** AI should not unfairly disadvantage people or groups. Biased training data can cause a system to treat some groups less fairly than others.

- **Privacy:** Personal information should be protected. Users should avoid sharing sensitive details, such as passwords or private documents, with AI tools without appropriate authorization.

- **Accuracy:** AI-generated information should be checked because AI systems can produce incorrect or misleading answers.

- **Transparency:** People should be honest about how they use AI, especially when a school or organization requires them to disclose AI assistance.

- **Accountability:** People and organizations must take responsibility for how AI systems are used and for the decisions they make based on AI outputs.

- **Academic integrity:** Students should follow their school's rules when using AI. AI can help explain concepts and generate practice questions, but students should not use it to cheat or misrepresent their work.

## Topic 6: Practical AI Skills

Knowing how to use an AI tool is not enough. Users must also know how to ask useful questions and evaluate the answers they receive.

- **Write clear prompts.** A prompt is the instruction or question you give an AI tool. Include enough detail about your goal, the context, and the type of answer you want.

  Example: Instead of asking, "Explain physics," ask, "Explain Newton's second law in simple English and give one everyday example."

- **Verify claims using reliable sources.** Check important facts against textbooks, academic research, or official websites. Make sure that references provided by AI actually exist and support the claims.

- **Check relevance.** Make sure the answer directly addresses your question instead of providing unrelated information.

- **Check completeness.** Determine whether the answer covers all the important parts of your question or leaves out necessary information.

- **Check for bias.** Consider whether the answer unfairly favors or disadvantages a person or group, or presents only one side of an issue.

- **Follow school rules.** Use AI in ways permitted by your instructor and disclose AI assistance when required.
