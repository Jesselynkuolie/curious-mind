### 4.2 Team Bumingbai Review

The Team Bumingbai review provided scores and specific comments.

| Deliverable | Score |
|---|---:|
| Study Guide and Test Bank | 14/20 |
| PowerPoint Slides | 13/20 |
| Video Tutorials | 14/20 |

**Study Guide findings**

- Activities 1.2 and 2.1 were useful for explaining rule-based systems and classification metrics.
- Worked answers and numerical examples were generally accurate.
- The SWISH example on page 18 was marked as not tested.
- Author notes and placeholders remained on several pages.
- The AI-assistance statement on page 68 was incomplete.
- Activity 1.6 revealed its answer inside the task.
- Some activity references and cross-references were incorrect.
- The guide needed more diagrams and visual explanations.

**Test Bank findings**

- The test bank contained 52 questions across six chapters.
- The answer key and sample open-ended answers were considered accurate.
- Section B had an uneven distribution of correct answers, with option B appearing too frequently.
- Some incorrect options were too obviously wrong.
- One question referred to an activity that did not exist under the stated name.
- Questions about the three AI approaches and precision and recall were missing.
- Open-ended question word-count instructions were inconsistent.

**PowerPoint findings**

- The presentation had a consistent visual theme.
- The diagram on slide 5 and critical-thinking activity on slide 14 were identified as strengths.
- Slide 7 interrupted the earlier chapter sequence.
- Some technical terms and diagrams needed clearer explanations.
- The diagram on slide 9 did not fully match its written explanation.
- Slide 11 contained a spelling error and alignment issues.
- Some infographic labels were too small.
- Image credits were missing from slides 6, 10, and 12.
- The reference list on slide 16 needed review.

**Video tutorial findings**

- Narration was generally clear.
- Video B used a quiz-show format, while Video C included a practical prompt-improvement demonstration.
- Video B contained a narration and on-screen answer contradiction around 2:52–3:00.
- Some quiz questions needed more thinking time.
- Video A revealed an answer too early from 4:41 onward.
- Video C's objectives slide and narration did not match.
- An unrelated image appeared in Video C around 2:08–2:15.
- The videos mainly covered Chapters 5–6.
- Video templates, resolution, and audio needed greater consistency.

## 7. Visual and Accessibility Audit

The teacher and peer reviewers identified a need for stronger visual support.

The team should verify that:

- Important concepts have suitable diagrams or illustrations.
- Images are relevant to the explanations.
- Text and labels are readable.
- Image sources and credits are included.
- Videos include subtitles where required.
- Visuals support learning rather than simply decorate the materials.
- Markdown images display correctly in GitHub.

**Current limitation:** The repository snapshot reviewed for this audit did not contain a complete separate collection of visual assets. The team must check the latest repository before claiming that all visual requirements have been completed.
