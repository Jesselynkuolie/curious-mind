## 3. Resource Descriptions

### A. Markdown Chapters

The six Markdown chapters provide the main learning content of the module.

They introduce the topics in a logical sequence, from basic AI concepts to practical AI skills.

### B. Study Guide

The Study Guide supports independent learning and revision.

It should provide:
- Important definitions and terminology.
- Summaries of the main concepts.
- Explanations of key topics.
- Relevant examples and learning points.

### C. Test Bank

The Test Bank allows learners to check their understanding.

It includes different types of questions and activities, such as:
- True or False questions.
- Multiple-choice questions.
- Open-ended missions.
- Practical application questions.

### D. PowerPoint Presentation

The PowerPoint Presentation supports teaching and classroom explanations.

It should highlight important concepts, provide examples, and use appropriate visuals to support understanding.

### E. Video Tutorials

The Video Tutorials provide additional explanations and demonstrations.

They should help learners understand difficult concepts through narration, examples, and visual explanations.
