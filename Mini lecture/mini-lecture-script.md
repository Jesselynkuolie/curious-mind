## Opening

Hello everyone. Today, we will introduce artificial
intelligence, or AI. We encounter AI in recommendation systems,
navigation apps, spam filters, and tools that generate text
or images. But what does AI mean, and why should we use it
carefully?

## What Is AI?

AI is a field of computer science focused on systems that
perform tasks associated with intelligent behavior, such as
recognizing patterns, processing language, and making predictions.

AI does not think exactly like a human, and its answers are
not always correct.

## Machine Learning

Machine learning is one approach within AI. A model learns
patterns from data.

For example, a spam filter may learn from emails labeled
"spam" or "not spam." Data quality matters because incomplete
or unrepresentative data can lead to poor results.

## Generative AI

Generative AI can create text, images, audio, or code.
Large language models work with language and commonly generate
text by predicting likely next tokens.

A specific prompt can improve relevance, but it does not
guarantee that the answer is accurate.

## Responsible Use

AI can save time and help people explore ideas, but it may
produce inaccurate information, reflect bias, or raise privacy
concerns.

Avoid sharing sensitive information in unapproved tools.
Check important facts using reliable sources and follow school
rules for AI assistance.

## Closing

AI can be useful when we understand its purpose and limitations.
Ask clear questions, think critically, verify important
information, and take responsibility for how you use the results.
